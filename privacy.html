TripBill Privacy Policy

Effective: 14 November 2026

TripBill is made by Thang Ly ("we"). This policy covers the TripBill apps for iPhone, iPad and Android.

Short version

We don't collect any data. TripBill has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What stays on your device

Trips, people, payments, exchange rates and settings are stored only on your device. We can't see them and we don't have a copy.

Contacts

When you add a person from your contacts, TripBill uses the system contact picker. The app only gets the name of the contact you pick, and it doesn't ask for access to your whole address book. That name is saved on your device only.

Online exchange rates

If "Suggest online rates" is on, TripBill asks the free Frankfurter service (api.frankfurter.dev) for an exchange rate. The request contains only the two currency codes. Like any website, that service can see your IP address. TripBill sends nothing else. You can turn this off in Settings and type rates yourself.

Backup and export files

Backup files (.tripbill) and reports are created only when you ask for them, and they go wherever you choose to share or save them. We never receive them.

Children

TripBill doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Delete a trip in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
