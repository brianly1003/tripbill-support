Common questions

Do I need an account or internet? No. Everything works offline. Internet is only used to suggest exchange rates, and you can always type a rate yourself.

How do I move my trips to a new phone? Settings → Back up all trips, save the file, then Settings → Restore from file on the new phone. It works between iPhone, iPad and Android.

How do I share a trip with a friend? Open the trip, tap ⋮ → Share trip file. They open the file in TripBill.

Where is my data? Only on your device. See the Privacy Policy.
